About Rui Cunha, Broker and Founder of Royal Star Corp

Updated October 6, 2026

Rui "Roy" Cunha is the broker and founder of Royal Star Corp, a real estate brokerage in Deerfield Beach, Florida. He has worked in South Florida real estate since 2003, first in mortgage lending, then in sales, and later in construction. When you work with Rui, you work with one person who understands how a home gets financed, built, bought and sold.

From mortgages to brokerage

Rui started in 2003 as a mortgage broker, and in 2005 he founded his own mortgage brokerage, Capax Corp. He earned his Florida real estate license in 2006. In 2010 he became a licensed broker and opened Royal Star Corp, where he is still the broker in charge. In 2018 he also became a licensed General Contractor, operating as Capax Construction.

Before real estate, Rui was an executive at American Express in Brazil and in the United States, working in technology and marketing (CRM). He holds bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and in Marketing.

Why that mix matters to you

Most buyers and sellers deal with a separate person for every step. Rui's background lets him see the whole picture:

  • Financing. Years as a mortgage broker mean he knows what lenders look at and can help you prepare, including for ITIN mortgages. Final approval always comes from the lender.
  • Construction. As a General Contractor, he can tell you which repairs actually matter before you sell your home, and what an inspection finding might really cost when you buy a home.
  • Two countries. Rui speaks Portuguese and English and knows how decisions get made both in Brazil and in Florida. That helps international buyers as much as families who already live here.

Where Rui works

Royal Star Corp is based in Deerfield Beach and serves clients all over Florida, from Miami, Fort Lauderdale and Boca Raton to Orlando, Tampa, Naples, Jacksonville and Key West. See the Florida cities we serve.

How he works

Straight answers, real numbers, no sales pitch. If something doesn't make sense for you, Rui will say so. For tax, legal or immigration questions, he'll point you to the right professional rather than guess.

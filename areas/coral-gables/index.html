Coral Gables Homes for Sale: Historic Streets, Strict Rules, Practical Advice

Updated October 6, 2026

Homes for sale in Coral Gables are mostly single-family houses, from 1920s Mediterranean Revival homes on tree-lined streets to gated bayfront estates, plus a smaller condo market near downtown and Miracle Mile. It's one of the most expensive cities in Miami-Dade, and one of the most regulated: almost anything you change on the outside of a house needs design approval. Rui Cunha's office is in Deerfield Beach, and he works with buyers across Florida, including Coral Gables.

What it's like to live in Coral Gables

Coral Gables is a planned city just southwest of downtown Miami. Florida's Office of Economic and Demographic Research estimated its population at 50,813 as of April 2024 (Florida EDR). It was developed by George Merrick during the Florida land boom of the 1920s, its architecture is almost entirely Mediterranean Revival as the original plan required, and it's known as "The City Beautiful" (Wikipedia). Landmarks include the Biltmore Hotel (opened 1926), the Venetian Pool and Miracle Mile, and the University of Miami is the city's largest employer. Several countries also have consulates here.

Neighborhoods

A local brokerage highlights these areas (Miami Signature Homes):

  • The Biltmore area. Historic homes in Mediterranean Revival and Colonial styles near the hotel and golf course.
  • Coral Gables Country Club area. Established streets close to the city's golf courses.
  • Downtown Coral Gables. A walkable area with dining, galleries and shops.
  • Cocoplum and Gables Estates. Gated waterfront communities with large estates and access to Biscayne Bay.

The gap between a downtown condo, a 1920s house and a bayfront estate is enormous, so citywide numbers only go so far. Rui can pull recent sales street by street.

Coral Gables market snapshot

According to Zillow, the typical home value in Coral Gables was about $1.56 million in August 2026, up about 7% from a year earlier (Zillow Home Value Index). Miller Samuel's Q4 2025 report put the single-family median at $1,915,000, essentially unchanged from a year earlier, with houses taking about 92 days to sell; the condo median was $530,000, down about 8% (Miller Samuel).

So houses have held their value, while condos have been softer. Prices vary widely by lot, age, renovation and water access. Ask Rui for a current comparison of recent sales for what you're actually considering.

Practical notes: design rules, flood, insurance and condos

Board of Architects. Coral Gables reviews a wide range of projects to preserve the city's traditional look: new houses, pools, paving, signs, roof tiles and building expansions, judged on color, materials, windows and proportion (City of Coral Gables). Historic properties also fall under the city's historic preservation rules (Wikipedia). If you plan to renovate, budget time and money for design review before you buy, and check whether the house is historically designated.

Flood and insurance. Under FEMA's newer pricing, the biggest flood premium increases in Miami-Dade were expected in the southern part of the county, including the Coral Gables ZIP code 33146 (WLRN). Citizens policyholders must carry flood coverage as that requirement phases in through 2027 (News4JAX). Older houses can also be costly to insure, so get real quotes and ask about the roof's age before signing.

Condos. Florida's 2022 condo safety law requires milestone inspections for buildings of three or more stories (at 30 years, or 25 if the local building department requires it, then every 10 years — SB 154, 2023) and a Structural Integrity Reserve Study with funded reserves (Rimkus). Ask for the reserve study, budget and assessment history before making an offer.

Homestead. If the home will be your primary residence, ask a CPA about Florida's homestead exemption. Second homes and non-residents don't qualify.

Schools

Coral Gables is served by Miami-Dade County Public Schools, and the University of Miami is in the city. Confirm assigned schools for any address with the district's school locator, and check grades with the Florida Department of Education.

Getting around

Coral Gables sits between downtown Miami and the southern suburbs, close to Miami International Airport. US-1 and Metrorail run along its eastern edge. Traffic can be heavy at rush hour, so drive your commute before you choose a street.

FAQ

How much does a home cost in Coral Gables?

Zillow put the typical home value at about $1.56 million in August 2026. Miller Samuel's Q4 2025 report put the single-family median at $1,915,000 and the condo median at $530,000.

Do I need approval to renovate a house in Coral Gables?

Usually, yes. The city's Board of Architects reviews new homes, pools, paving, roof tiles and expansions, among other projects, and historic properties have extra rules. Plan for review time before you start.

Is flood insurance expensive in Coral Gables?

It can be. FEMA's newer pricing was expected to bring some of the biggest premium increases in Miami-Dade to southern areas including Coral Gables. Check the flood zone and get quotes before signing.

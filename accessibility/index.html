Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Royal Star Corp wants everyone to be able to use royalstarcorp.com, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. In practice, that means things like:

  • text alternatives for meaningful images;
  • enough color contrast to read comfortably;
  • a site you can use with a keyboard alone, with visible focus;
  • clear headings and labels, and forms that work with screen readers;
  • pages that adapt to phones and to enlarged text.

Known limitations

Some content may come from third parties, such as embedded videos, maps or listing data, and may not fully meet these standards. We're working to improve what we control.

What happens after you message

  1. Rui reads your message himself and replies, usually the same business day.
  2. He'll ask a few quick questions: are you buying or selling, where, your timeline, and how you plan to pay or finance.

Helpful to have ready

  • Buying: the areas you like, your rough budget, and whether you're paying cash or financing (and if you already have a pre-approval or an ITIN).

  • Investing from abroad: where you live now and whether you'd buy in your own name or through a company. Tax questions go to a CPA, and Rui can tell you what to ask.

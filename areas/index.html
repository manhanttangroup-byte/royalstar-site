Florida Cities We Serve

Updated October 6, 2026

Royal Star Corp's office is in Deerfield Beach, but Rui Cunha works with buyers, sellers and investors all over Florida. The guides below are grouped by region. Each one covers what it's like to live there, the kinds of homes you'll find, recent prices with sources where we have them, and the practical issues buyers often miss, like condo assessments, flood zones and insurance.

Miami-Dade

  • Miami: downtown and Brickell high-rises, established neighborhoods and a big international buyer market.
  • Miami Beach: oceanfront condos and island living, where building age and insurance matter a lot.
  • Aventura: condo towers and shopping, with quick access to both Miami and Broward.
  • Coral Gables: tree-lined streets, older single-family homes and strict city building rules.
  • Doral: newer communities and townhomes close to Miami International Airport.
  • Sunny Isles Beach: beachfront condo towers popular with buyers from abroad.

Broward

  • Deerfield Beach: beach condos, 55+ communities and inland neighborhoods, and home to our office.
  • Pompano Beach: beach and Intracoastal living and a large Brazilian community.
  • Fort Lauderdale: downtown, canals and high-rise condos.
  • Hollywood: beach, Broadwalk and older neighborhoods between Fort Lauderdale and Miami.
  • Weston: planned suburban communities in western Broward.
  • Coconut Creek: family suburbs, gated communities and 55+ sections.
  • Margate: more affordable inland homes and condos.
  • Parkland: gated communities and larger single-family homes.
  • Lighthouse Point: deep-water canal homes near the Hillsboro Inlet.
  • Lauderdale-By-The-Sea: a small beach town of condos and low-rise homes.

Palm Beach

  • Boca Raton: golf and country-club communities, 55+ condos and beaches.
  • Delray Beach: a walkable downtown on Atlantic Avenue and 55+ communities to the west.
  • Boynton Beach: 55+ communities, family homes and coastal neighborhoods.
  • West Palm Beach: downtown condos, historic neighborhoods and the county's business center.

Central Florida

  • Orlando: theme parks, a growing metro area and a favorite region for Brazilian buyers.
  • Kissimmee: vacation-home communities near the parks, where short-term rental rules vary by community.

Tampa Bay and the Gulf Coast

  • Tampa: a large metro with urban neighborhoods and growing suburbs.
  • St. Petersburg: a waterfront downtown with Gulf beaches nearby.
  • Sarasota: Gulf beaches, an arts scene and many retirees.
  • Naples: golf communities, Gulf-front homes and a high-end market.

North Florida

  • Jacksonville: beaches, river neighborhoods and suburbs in Northeast Florida.

Florida Keys

  • Key West: an island town with historic homes, where flood insurance and building rules weigh heavily.

What changes from county to county

The county decides your school district and property appraiser, and tax rates differ. In South Florida, for example, one broker summary put Palm Beach County's 2025 total millage slightly below Broward's (Momentum), though actual bills depend on the city. Coastal areas face higher flood-insurance pressure than inland ones (WLRN). Not sure where to start? Contact Rui.

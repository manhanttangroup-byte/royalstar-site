Who Rui helps

Local buyers and sellers

Whether you're buying your first condo in Deerfield Beach, moving up to a single-family home in Coconut Creek, or selling a house you've owned for years, Rui works the same streets you live on. His office is on NW 9th Avenue in Deerfield Beach. Sellers can start with a free market valuation on the sell your home page.

Immigrants and newcomers, including Brazilians

Florida has the largest Brazilian-born population of any US state, and Broward is the Florida county where most of them live (Migration Policy Institute, 2026). Many newcomers have steady income but no Social Security number or US credit history yet. Some lenders offer ITIN mortgages for that situation, usually with a larger down payment and a higher rate than a conventional loan, and always subject to lender approval. Rui's years as a mortgage broker help you see early on which path fits your documents.

International buyers

Buyers who live abroad can purchase Florida property without US residency, and much of the process can be done remotely. Rui walks international buyers through financing for foreign nationals, the ITIN, taxes when you later sell, and the real monthly costs of owning here. Buying property does not give anyone a visa or a green card. That's a separate legal process.

How it works

  1. A first conversation. Tell Rui what you want to buy or sell, your timeline and your budget, on WhatsApp, by phone or in person. No cost, no pressure.
  2. Numbers before houses. For buyers, that means a lender pre-approval and a realistic monthly payment, including taxes, insurance and HOA or condo fees. Try the mortgage calculator for a first estimate. For sellers, it means a price range based on recent nearby sales.
  3. The search or the listing. Rui shows you homes that fit or puts your home on the MLS with good photos and a clear description, in front of English- and Portuguese-speaking buyers.
  4. Offer, inspection and negotiation. This is where his contractor background helps: he can tell you which inspection findings are serious and which are routine.
  5. Closing. Rui works with the lender and the title company until the keys change hands, and stays available after closing for questions.

FAQ

Is Rui Cunha a licensed real estate broker?

Yes. Rui has held a Florida real estate license since 2006 and a broker license since 2010, and he is the broker and founder of Royal Star Corp in Deerfield Beach. He has also been a licensed General Contractor since 2018.

Can I work with Royal Star Corp in Portuguese?

Yes. Rui is fluent in Portuguese and English, so showings, walking through the contract and calls with lenders can happen in either language. The Portuguese version of this site is at /pt/.

Can I buy a home in Florida without a Social Security number?

Often, yes. Some lenders offer ITIN mortgages for borrowers who file US taxes with an ITIN instead of an SSN. Expect a larger down payment and a higher rate than a conventional loan, and approval is always up to the lender. See the ITIN mortgage guide.

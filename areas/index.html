South Florida Cities We Know Best

Updated October 6, 2026

Royal Star Corp is based in Deerfield Beach, and these six cities are where Rui Cunha works most: five in Broward County and Boca Raton in Palm Beach County. Each guide below covers what it's like to live there, the communities, recent prices with sources, and the practical issues buyers often miss, like condo assessments, flood zones and insurance.

How Rui helps you choose

Rui has been in South Florida real estate since 2003, as a mortgage broker, a broker and a licensed General Contractor. He'll compare recent sales for the exact type of home you want, help you read a condo's reserve study, and run the monthly numbers with taxes and insurance included, in English or Portuguese. Start with our buyer's guide or the mortgage calculator.

Broward or Palm Beach?

Boca Raton is in Palm Beach County; the other five cities are in Broward. The county decides your school district and property appraiser, and tax rates differ: one broker summary put Palm Beach County's 2025 total millage slightly below Broward's (Momentum), though actual bills depend on the city. Coastal areas in both counties face higher flood-insurance pressure than inland ones (WLRN). Not sure where to start? Contact Rui.

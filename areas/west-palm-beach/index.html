West Palm Beach Homes for Sale: Downtown Condos, Historic Homes and Practical Advice

Updated October 8, 2026

Homes for sale in West Palm Beach include condos downtown and along the Intracoastal, older houses in historic districts, and newer single-family homes farther west. Right now the two markets are moving in different directions: house prices rose over the past year while condo prices dipped, and condo buyers have plenty of choice. Rui Cunha's office is in Coral Springs, and he works with buyers across Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach.

What it's like to live in West Palm Beach

West Palm Beach is the county seat and business center of Palm Beach County, on the Intracoastal Waterway (the Lake Worth Lagoon) across from the town of Palm Beach. Florida's Office of Economic and Demographic Research estimated its population at 125,319 as of April 2024, up 2.6% in a year (Florida EDR).

Downtown, around Clematis Street and the waterfront, has condo towers, offices, restaurants and a Brightline station with train service to Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Orlando (Brightline). Palm Beach International Airport is also in the city. Away from downtown, the city is mostly residential, from historic neighborhoods near the water to suburban areas to the west.

Neighborhoods and communities

West Palm Beach has several historic districts on the National Register of Historic Places (Wikipedia, NRHP list), including:

  • El Cid. A historic district on the waterfront, listed in 1995.
  • Flamingo Park. A historic residential district, listed in 2000.
  • Grandview Heights. A historic district listed in 1999.
  • Old Northwood. A historic district north of downtown, listed in 1994.
  • Prospect Park–Southland Park. A historic district listed in 2011.

Downtown and the waterfront are mostly condo towers. If you buy in a historic district, ask whether exterior changes, windows or additions need approval from the city's historic preservation office before you plan a renovation. Prices vary a lot block by block, so Rui can pull recent sales for the exact streets you like.

West Palm Beach market snapshot

PBP Real Estate's analysis of BeachesMLS data shows a median house price of $625,000 in West Palm Beach in September 2026, up 15.7% from September 2025. The median condo or townhouse price was $210,000, down 5.4% (PBP Real Estate, updated October 2026).

The same report counted about 4.9 months of house inventory and 7.8 months for condos and townhomes, and roughly 4 in 10 homes for sale had cut their price. One month of data can swing a lot, and a downtown tower unit, a historic bungalow and a newer house out west are very different markets. Ask Rui for recent sales of the kind of home you actually want.

Practical notes: condos, flood, taxes and rentals

Condo buildings. Florida's condo safety laws require milestone inspections for buildings of three or more stories (at 30 years, or 25 if the local building department requires it, then every 10 years — SB 154, 2023) and a Structural Integrity Reserve Study with funded reserves (Rimkus). Owners pay for required repairs, sometimes through special assessments. Ask for the reserve study, budget, inspection reports and assessment history before you make an offer.

Flood and insurance. Homes near the Intracoastal and in low-lying areas carry more flood risk. FEMA's newer pricing raised flood premiums most in coastal Broward and Palm Beach (WLRN), and Citizens policyholders must carry flood coverage as that requirement phases in through 2027 (News4JAX). Get real quotes before you sign.

Taxes. West Palm Beach is in Palm Beach County, so the property appraiser, tax rates and school district differ from Broward. Owners who make the home their primary residence may qualify for the homestead exemption, which also caps yearly increases in assessed value (Palm Beach County). Ask a CPA how this applies to you.

Short-term rentals. In Palm Beach County, hosts must register for and pay the county's tourist development tax themselves; the Tax Collector says booking platforms do not collect it for the county (PBC Tax Collector). The city and many condo associations have their own rules, so check both before you buy a property to rent.

Schools

West Palm Beach is served by the School District of Palm Beach County, not Broward. Confirm the assigned schools for any address with the district and check school grades with the Florida Department of Education.

Getting around

I-95 and Florida's Turnpike run through the city, and Brightline connects downtown to South Florida and Orlando. Traffic on I-95 can be heavy at rush hour, so test your commute before you commit.

FAQ

What is the median home price in West Palm Beach?

PBP Real Estate's analysis of BeachesMLS data put the median house price at $625,000 in September 2026, up 15.7% from a year earlier. The median condo or townhouse price was $210,000, down 5.4%.

Are West Palm Beach condo prices falling?

Condo and townhouse prices were down about 5% year over year in September 2026, with nearly 8 months of inventory. Older buildings can face large inspection and reserve costs, so check each building's reserves and assessment history.

Is West Palm Beach in Broward or Palm Beach County?

Palm Beach County. That means a different school district, property appraiser and tax rates than Broward cities like Deerfield Beach or Fort Lauderdale.

Sunny Isles Beach Homes for Sale: Oceanfront Condos, Explained

Updated October 8, 2026

Homes for sale in Sunny Isles Beach are almost all condos, in towers lined up between the ocean and the Intracoastal, from older mid-rise buildings to new luxury towers; the city's skyline has been ranked among the tallest in the United States (Wikipedia). In a city like this, you're buying a building as much as a unit: its reserves, inspections, insurance and rental rules shape your monthly cost. Rui Cunha's office is in Coral Springs, and he works with buyers across Florida, including Sunny Isles Beach.

What it's like to live in Sunny Isles Beach

Sunny Isles Beach is a small city on a barrier island in northeast Miami-Dade, between the Atlantic Ocean and the Intracoastal Waterway, with Collins Avenue (A1A) as its main road (Wikipedia). Florida's Office of Economic and Demographic Research estimated its population at 22,788 as of April 2024 (Florida EDR). The city incorporated in 1997, and over the following years high-rise condos and hotels replaced most of the old motels along the beach. It's a very international community: Russian was the most reported ancestry in 2020 data, at about 15%, followed by Cuban, Colombian and Venezuelan (Wikipedia).

Buildings and areas

Sunny Isles is less about neighborhoods and more about buildings:

  • Oceanfront towers. Buildings on the ocean side of Collins Avenue, with direct beach access.
  • Intracoastal side. Buildings on the west side of Collins, facing the bay and the Intracoastal.
  • Older buildings. Older condos still stand among the newer towers; they need careful review under Florida's condo safety rules.

Each building has its own age, budget, insurance and rules. Rui can pull recent sales and compare buildings side by side.

Sunny Isles Beach market snapshot

According to Zillow, the typical home value in Sunny Isles Beach was about $651,000 in August 2026, about 1% higher than a year earlier (Zillow Home Value Index). Broader reports on the Miami beach market point the same way for condos: Corcoran's second-quarter 2026 report put the condo median for the Miami beaches at about $630,000, down about 4% year over year, with condo inventory down 17% (Corcoran, 2Q 2026), and Miller Samuel's Q4 2025 report for Miami Beach and the barrier islands found homes taking about 132 days to sell (Miller Samuel).

Those regional figures cover a much wider area than Sunny Isles, and prices inside the city range from older units to multimillion-dollar penthouses. Ask Rui for a current comparison of recent sales in the buildings you're considering.

Practical notes: condos, flood and rentals

Condo safety rules. Florida tightened its condo laws after the 2021 collapse of Champlain Towers South in neighboring Surfside (Wikipedia). Buildings of three or more stories now need milestone inspections (at 30 years, or 25 if the local building department requires it, then every 10 years — SB 154, 2023) and a Structural Integrity Reserve Study with funded reserves (Rimkus). Special assessments from $10,000 to over $100,000 have been reported (24-7 Press Release). Ask for the reserve study, budget, inspection reports and assessment history before you offer.

Flood and insurance. Under FEMA's newer pricing, Sunny Isles is among the most expensive places in South Florida to insure against flooding (WLRN), and Citizens policyholders must carry flood coverage as that requirement phases in through 2027 (News4JAX). In a condo, ask what the association's master policy covers and what you need to insure yourself.

Short-term rentals. Renting a condo or apartment for short stays requires a Sunny Isles Beach short-term vacation rental license for each unit, written consent from the condo association (or bylaws that allow it), a state lodging license and tax registrations with Florida and Miami-Dade (City of Sunny Isles Beach). The association can say no, so read the bylaws first.

Homestead. If the home will be your primary residence, ask a CPA about Florida's homestead exemption. Second homes and non-residents don't qualify.

Schools

Sunny Isles Beach is served by Miami-Dade County Public Schools, and the city's own public school is Norman S. Edelcup/Sunny Isles Beach K-8 (Wikipedia). Confirm assigned schools for any address with the district's school locator and check grades with the Florida Department of Education.

Getting around

Collins Avenue runs the length of the city, with Aventura's shopping just across the Intracoastal and Broward County a short drive north. Traffic on Collins and the causeways can be slow at peak times, so test your commute.

FAQ

How much does a condo cost in Sunny Isles Beach?

Zillow put the typical home value at about $651,000 in August 2026, about 1% higher than a year earlier. Prices range widely, from older units to oceanfront penthouses, so compare recent sales in the building you want.

Can I rent my Sunny Isles condo on Airbnb?

Only if your building allows it and you get the city's short-term vacation rental license, plus state and county registrations. Associations can refuse short stays, so check the bylaws before you buy.

What should I check before buying a Sunny Isles condo?

Ask for the reserve study, the current budget, the milestone inspection status, the history of special assessments, the master insurance policy and the leasing rules.
